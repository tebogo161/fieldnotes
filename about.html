Hello, I’m Katlego

Technology makes the everyday possible. I want to understand how.

I’m a first-year Computer Systems Engineering student at Botswana Accountancy College. I’ve always been interested in computers and the systems behind them: how they work, how they connect, and how thoughtful technology can help solve real problems.

College has introduced me to new ideas and a new level of independence. I’m learning that progress takes more than attending class. It takes practice, questions, patience and the willingness to try again when something doesn’t click the first time.

This site is my record of that process. It brings together reflections on learning and teamwork, early projects, and a few study habits that are helping me along the way.
